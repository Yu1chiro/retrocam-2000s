Eight lenses, none from this decade.

Every lens is processed live in the viewfinder, so what you see is exactly what you get. Each shot gets its own light leak and a few specks of dust.

Everything a 2003 camera had, plus a few things it never did.

Date stamp
Glowing orange digits burned into the corner of every shot, just like the old days. Switch it off whenever you like.
Photo and video
Change modes from the menu. Video keeps the lens look and records sound if you allow the microphone.
Zoom
Use the slider, the plus and minus keys, the mouse wheel, or pinch on your phone.
Formats
Shoot in 3:4, 4:5, 1:1, 9:16, 4:3 or 16:9 for every kind of screen.
Exposure
Dim it down or brighten it up with a single slider.
Gallery and sharing
Every shot is saved to a gallery on your device. Share it straight to your socials or download it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Allow the camera

    Your browser asks once. Microphone access is optional and only needed for video.

  2. 2

    Pick a lens and format

    Open the menu for brightness, video mode and permissions.

  3. 3

    Press the red button

    Watch the print develop, then find it waiting in your gallery.
